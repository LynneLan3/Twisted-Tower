# Editorial Method

This site follows an evidence-first editorial method.

## Source priority for facts

1. Official channels: Steam store page, official launch announcements, developer press releases
2. Reliable published launch reviews and media coverage
3. Player community information (used only to discover demand, never directly as a source of facts)

## Evidence boundaries

- Every factual claim is attributed to a source; content without sufficient sourcing is not published.
- Anything not confirmed by official or reliable sources (specific enemy names, boss tactics, hidden-collectible lists) does not enter public pages.
- Content marked as "review-based" on a page is explicitly labeled as coming from reviews rather than official material.
- Content marked as "community completion runs" on a page is guide advice (how to check progress, how to spend resources), not game fact; its source nature is labeled on the page.
- If official information changes (new platforms, patch changes), pages are updated and dated.

## What we will not do

- We do not invent characters, enemies, items, mechanics, or numbers.
- We do not publish filler content written only to add length.
- We do not publish unsupported rankings such as "best weapon" or "all bosses."
