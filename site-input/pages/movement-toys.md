Twisted Tower includes many "toys" used for movement. The official store page confirms that collecting toys grants **jumping, dashing, and grappling** movement; official achievement descriptions further confirm four named movement toys. Movement toys let you reach high spots and secret areas, and are key to advancing some floor routes.

## Officially confirmed movement toys

The official achievements page confirms these four named movement toys:

| Toy | Official achievement description |
| --- | --- |
| Harpoon | "Snatch that handy Harpoon!" |
| Fairy Wings | "Fly high with the swift Fairy Wings!" |
| Bouncy Boots | "Strap on those Bouncy Boots, Chap, and bounce your way to bigger thrills!" |
| Twisted Jet Pack | "Collect the Twisted Jet Pack!" |

(Source: Steam Community official achievements page)

## How movement works

- The official store page says collecting these "strange toys" grants movement options such as **jumping, dashing, and grappling**.
- Some movement toys are hidden behind side rooms and puzzles, so exploring pays off (source: reviews noting hidden areas and collectibles).
- Movement toys help reach high spots and secret areas that are otherwise out of reach — the official "Secret Spectacle" and "Secret Safari" achievements confirm a hidden-area system.

## Tips for finding and using them

The following are suggestions compiled from community completion runs, not official mechanics:

- **Test a new toy right after picking it up**: after the pickup prompt completes, try the new ability at the nearest landmark to confirm it works before hunting for its use spots.
- **Check whether a toy is this floor's progress condition**: some movement toys unlock the floor's elevator — after grabbing one, recheck high ledges and gaps you couldn't reach before; others are optional collectibles that don't block the story.
- **Combine toys**: grapple, dash, and jump together reach some upper platforms and hidden areas — look up while exploring.

(Sources: community completion runs; see the [editorial method](/editorial-method/).)
