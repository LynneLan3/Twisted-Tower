Official release and platform information for Twisted Tower, covering launch date, price, platforms, and language support.

## Release information

- **Release date**: August 18, 2026
- **Platform**: PC (Windows), sold through Steam
- **Price**: $14.99; a 10% launch discount ($13.49) ran through September 1, 2026
- **Language**: English (interface / audio / subtitles)

## Platform status

- The game is currently on Steam (PC) only; as of 2026-08-21 no PS5, Xbox, or Switch version has been announced (sources: Steam store page / PlayFront).
- No DLC has been announced.
- A free demo (Steam App ID 2506090) existed around launch and was retired with the full release (sources: official announcement, community threads).
- A day-one 1.0.3 update shipped at release, with follow-up versions promised; the game continues to receive updates post-launch (source: official day-one announcement).

## Frequently asked questions

- **Will there be a console version?** The developer is currently focused on PC and has not announced console plans (source: PlayFront relaying the developer's statement).
- **Are other languages supported?** The store page currently lists English only.
- **What are the system requirements?** See the [system requirements page](/twisted-tower/system-requirements/).
- **How long is the game?** A story run is roughly 4–6 hours; see the [beginner's guide](/twisted-tower/beginner-guide/).
